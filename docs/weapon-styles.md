# Weapon styles and rage arts

Each shop weapon now fights its own way and has its own rage art. The owner's brief (2026-11-23): "each weapon should have unique combat styles, attacking patterns ... I should feel the difference between the combat in each weapon", a rage art per weapon "with VFX" and its name on the screen, and Shizuka's art reworked: "he will sheathe the sword ... then he cuts, then moves past him, and then the person falls down."

The second round (same day): "Iwakudaki is not designed yet: design it; Earthsplitter is its rage art: it should hit the opponent and crack the ground, and the entire screen should break like glass after the opponent has been killed, a big animation when triggered"; "make Hell Splitter a little fancier with more VFX"; "the hammer is not displayed properly: there is a gap in the weapon"; and the tiers: "a basic katana with no design as the common and starting weapon; there is only one common, then rare starts with Kagetsu, then epic, and the last two legendary with a rainbow gradient; design the legendary weapons' VFX accordingly".

Code: `Combat/Styles/` (`WeaponStyle`, `WeaponStyles`, `ShizukaMoves`, `HammerMoves`), `Combat/Fighter.Style.cs`, `Combat/Fighter.RageArts.cs`, `Combat/RageArts/Fighter.Art.*.cs`, `VFX/Vfx.RageArts.cs`, `VFX/Vfx.Earth.cs`, `VFX/WeaponCues.cs`, `VFX/RageArtCues.cs` (`RageArtLook`), `UI/Hud.RageArt.cs`, `UI/Hud.Shatter.cs`; the tiers and the plain starter in `Rules/GameRules.Gear.cs` and `Rules/JsTables.Lookup.cs`. Tests: `WeaponStyleTests`, `WeaponTierTests`, `WeaponDrawingTests`, `RageArtTests` (EditMode); `WeaponRageArtTests`, `WeaponStyleFightTests`, `GlassScreenTests` (PlayMode).

## The attack pattern system

A weapon's family (ADR-0006) still owns its moves, grip, blade and drawing. On top of it, a **weapon style** (`WeaponStyle`, one per shop weapon in `WeaponStyles`) owns the feel. It is small on purpose: every field is neutral at its default, a weapon with no style (every foe weapon, bare hands, the heavy tachi) fights exactly as its family, and every rule a style adds is read in one file, `Fighter.Style.cs`. Only the player's own equipped weapon takes its style (`Fighter.EquipWeapon`): a foe with no weapon of its own is handed its family's reference weapon (a Footman's "kagetsu") and must keep fighting exactly as its family does.

| Part | Fields | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tremor | `quakes`, `quakeRadius`, `quakeStun` | a move's impact frame shakes the floor: grounded foes near it are stunned, guarding or not (a jump clears it) |
| Tier | `legendary` | prismatic sparks on landed blows and dashes |
| Footwork | `moveMul`, `dashMul`, `footwork`, `heavyStep` | walk, run and dash speed; the dash's trail; thudding footfalls and landings |
| Tempo | `tempoPerHit`, `tempoMax`, `tempoHoldFrames`, `dashCancelOnHit` | momentum: landed blows speed up the next ones; a landed cut dash-cancels |
| Blows | `damageMul`, `postureMul`, `armouredSwings`, `guardCrush*`, `tip*`/`inner*`, `hook*` | weight, posture, armour, chip through a guard, the sweet spot, the hook mark |
| Defence | `riposteFrames`, `riposte` | after a guard or a parry, the Weapon button plays the Iai Counter, out of blockstun too |
| The scabbard | `echoes`, `echoDelay`, `MoveDef.sheathFrom/To` | the blade goes home; a silent cut opens on the foe a beat later |
| Buttons | `buildMoves` | a move set that plays some buttons differently over the family's (Shizuka) |
| Rage art | `rageArt` | the weapon's own art; null keeps the family's |

Each weapon gets one or two traits, chosen so no two weapons win the same way (`WeaponStyleTests.No_two_weapons_win_the_same_way`).

## The rage arts

Special + Down with full Rage, on the ground (ADR-0004). Every art except Thousand Shadows follows Crimson Moon's rules (ADR-0002): the world is held at scale 0 with Kage exempt from the first frame to the finisher, a cut-in band slides across with the art's name and kanji, the arena takes the art's deep colour (`Hud.ArtGrade`), the camera closes in through the rig's override, every blow is unblockable and lands only on the foe the art started on (the floor arts' blows also catch foes standing in them), and **the finisher's word is slammed onto the screen** (`Hud.ArtStamp`: the word in the brush font, the kanji large behind it, the art's name under it). Bare hands and the heavy tachi keep Thousand Shadows. With no foe (the training bag) the press is spent and the art announced, as before.

**The bosses read it** (`Fighter.RageArtCounter.cs`, `rageArtCounter.json`): against a sub-boss or Raikon every art has to be timed. It plays only when the boss is open as Kage starts it: reeling (a blow's hit-stun, a parry's stagger, a broken guard), thrown up by a blow, on the floor, or committed to an attack (wind-up to recovery). A boss in neutral, guarding, rolling or getting up, on invulnerable frames, or in a counter stance that catches Kage from the front turns it aside: the Rage is spent, no freeze or cut-in, Kage lunges in and reels 48 frames with no early way out, "RAGE COUNTERED" over him in PARRIED's red brush (`callout_rage_countered`, painted by `Tools/callout_art/make_rage_countered.py`), and the boss answers (its stance's own move, or its AI's answer to a parry). Every other foe takes the art wherever it stands. **The cue:** while Kage's Rage is full (and he is on his feet, not reeling) and the boss he targets is open, a small gold 怒 diamond (the RAGE READY pill's colours, a crimson beat behind it, a chevron pointing down) floats over the boss's head: press now. It pops in at each opening, breathes while it lasts and fades out when it closes (`Hud.RageCue.cs`, `HudRageCue.cs`, `HudReader.RageCue`).

## VFX briefs

The effects are procedural, in `Vfx.RageArts.cs`, built on the effekseer-vfx skill's craft rules the way `Vfx.cs` and the Raikon effects are (`docs/raikon-ascendant-vfx.md`): a time structure (anticipation, a 2–5 frame contact, a body that changes shape, secondary motion, dissipation), value layering (a white-hot core, a saturated body, a dark rim or residue in alpha blend), eased travel, and elements that differ in shape, motion and rhythm, not colour. Only the finisher of each art gets an impact flash.

| Element (art) | Core / body / dark | Shape read first | Motion and rhythm | Secondary and residue | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Violet (Silent Moon) | `#f0e6ff` / `#a46bff` / `#12052a` | one long thin line (well over 40:1) and thin lens cuts | stillness, then one instant; cuts open from the centre to the tips in 20 % of their life, then thin | petals that hang and fall only after the finisher; ink-violet residue under the line | a glowing blob; anything moving during the stillness |
| Lightning (Thousand Swallows) | `#e6f9ff` / `#38c8ff` / `#021626` | jagged bolts through the foe | does not travel; re-struck, a strike every 5 frames | afterimages that hold, then fade one by one; sparks | a straight line; bolts that slide |
| Hell fire (Hell Splitter) | `#ffd2a0` / `#ff3a1c` / `#2a0500` | the floor splitting, columns | heavy, ground-up; cracks grow out fast then cool from white to red to dark; pillars rise, hold, collapse | rock (dark) and embers thrown up, dark smoke rolling over, dust | fire that only scales and fades |

## Review images

`RageArtScreenshots` (PlayMode, needs a GPU: `-testFilter RageArtScreenshots` without `-nographics`) renders an eight-tile sheet per art into `Screenshots/review/weapon-arts/` (`silent_moon_sheet.png`, `thousand_swallows_sheet.png`, `hell_splitter_sheet.png`, `water_dragon_sheet.png`, `reapers_requiem_sheet.png`, and `tiles/`); Crimson Moon's and Thousand Shadows' (now with bare hands) stay in `Screenshots/review/SAN-82/`. The second round adds `earthsplitter_sheet.png`, `earthsplitter_kill_sheet.png` (a foe it kills: the shatter) and `look_iwakudaki.png`, `look_iwakudaki_raised.png`, `look_mumei.png`, `look_kagetsu.png` (close-ups of the weapons in Kage's hands). One round of fixes came from looking at them: a held foe no longer stays white (it can't fade a hit flash while the world is held, so it takes a faint tint of the art), the finisher's flash and rings are smaller, the word sits above the fighters, the lava and water columns keep their colour, and the water dragon carries the foe up instead of dropping it into a fall. The HUD now steps by the fixed capture time when one is set (`Hud.UiDeltaTime`: `Time.unscaledDeltaTime` ignores `Time.captureDeltaTime`), so the cut-in band, the stamp and the glass run at their real length in the sheets; the game never sets a capture time, so nothing changes there. The second round's fixes from the sheets:

- **The hammer's gap** (the owner's screenshot): a corner of Iwakudaki's head came out NaN (`Mathf.Sin(PI)` is a hair below zero in float, and a negative to the power 1.6 is NaN), and the broken polygon left a wedge of the head undrawn. Clamped at 0 (`CleaverVisual`, and the same expression in `Vfx.Lightning`). `WeaponDrawingTests` (EditMode) checks every shop weapon's drawing on Kage's rig is whole: finite vertices, and the main shapes' triangles cover exactly their outline's area.
- **The glass drew nothing**: its crack and shard graphics are classes nested in `Hud`, so Unity had no script asset to read `Graphic`'s `[RequireComponent(typeof(CanvasRenderer))]` from, and without a `CanvasRenderer` a graphic draws nothing, silently. They are created with one now. `GlassScreenTests` (PlayMode, needs a GPU, `-testFilter GlassScreenTests`) checks the crack lights the screen and the shatter carries the captured frame and is gone after.
- The smash's crack covered the whole screen as a regular yellow web for the rest of the art; it is a local, jagged star now, gone in 0.8 s, and the full web is the kill's.

## Back + Weapon counters (2026-11-27)

Every weapon now has a counter stance on Back + Weapon, as the katana's Mirror Guard always had. The stance catches a blow in frames 4-24; the blow is turned aside (the attacker reels as from a parry) and the weapon's riposte plays at once: fast, untouchable through its first frames, unblockable.

| Weapon (family) | Stance | Riposte |
|---|---|---|
| Kagetsu, Mumei, Shizuka (katana) | Mirror Guard | Kage Counter (teleports behind the attacker) |
| Mizuchi (spear) | Tide Turn (`spear.counterGuard`) | Tide Riposte, a sliding thrust (`spear.counterThrust`, 12) |
| Yamigama (chain sickle) | Reaper's Snare (`chain.counterGuard`) | Reaper's Reply, a reaping cut (`chain.counterReap`, 11) |
| Onikiri (cleaver) | Oni Wall (`cleaver.counterGuard`) | Oni Rebuke, a diagonal hack (`cleaver.counterHack`, 14) |
| Iwakudaki (hammer) | Anvil Guard (`iwakudaki.counterGuard`) | Anvil Rebound, a chopping slam (`iwakudaki.counterSmash`, 15) |

What Back + Weapon did before stays for the legacy (unprofiled) AI only: the spear's Raised Guard, the chain's Cyclone Chain Arc, the cleaver's sweep, the hammer's Rising Anvil (still on Up + Weapon). For the player the Cyclone Chain Arc has no button now. `WeaponCounterTests` (EditMode) fights each stance against a test blow, and Mikazuki against a foe.
